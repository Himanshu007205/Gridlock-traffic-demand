GRIDLOCK HACKATHON 2.0 — Traffic Demand Prediction
Final submission
========================================================================

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROBLEM OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Predict traffic demand (0–1 normalised) for specific geohash locations
at 15-minute timestamps on Day 49 of a multi-day dataset.  Training data
covers Days 48–49 (Day 49 only up to 2:00); the test set asks for
Day 49 from 2:15 onward, through 13:45.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORE INSIGHT / STRATEGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 48 is a near-perfect structural proxy for Day 49.  Instead of
predicting demand from scratch, we:

  1. Look up the exact Day-48 demand for the same (geohash, timestamp).
  2. Compute how the d49/d48 ratio is trending across hours 0, 1, 2
     (the only hours of Day 49 that are observable).
  3. Fit a linear trend on those three ratio observations per geohash
     and extrapolate it to the target test hour.
  4. Multiply the d48 demand by this extrapolated ratio.

The "ratio_at_hour" feature is consistently the single most important
predictor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
FEATURE ENGINEERING (57 features total)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

** Temporal Features **
- hour, minute, time_slot (0–95), time_of_day (fractional hour)
- Cyclical encodings: sin/cos of hour and time_slot
- is_peak flag  (07:00–10:00), is_night flag (22:00–05:00)
- day (48 or 49)

** Spatial Features **
- Latitude and longitude decoded from geohash (via pygeohash)
- Label-encoded geohash, geo3, geo4, geo5 prefixes

** Road / Environment Features **
- LargeVehicles allowed (binary), Landmarks present (binary)
- RoadType ordinal (Residential=0, Street=1, Highway=2)
- Weather ordinal (Sunny=0, Rainy=1, Foggy=2, Snowy=3)
- NumberofLanes, Temperature (median-filled per geohash)

** Cross-Day Core Features (strongest signals) **
- d48_demand          : exact d48 lookup at (geohash, timestamp)
- d48_hr_curr/prev    : d48 demand by hour (fallback when ts missing)
- d49_same_hr         : d49 demand at same hour (available for h0–2)
- d49_prev_hr         : d49 demand one hour earlier

** Slot-Level D48 Temporal Smoothing **
A single 15-min slot in d48 can be noisy.  We look up the ±1 slot
neighbours and compute a 3-slot rolling mean:
- d48_prev_slot       : d48 demand 15 min before the target slot
- d48_next_slot       : d48 demand 15 min after the target slot
- d48_roll3_mean      : mean(prev, curr, next) — stable baseline
- d48_roll3_hratio_pred : roll3_mean × ratio_at_hour (best combined rule)

** D49 Slot-Level Anchors **
D49 has 15-min granularity too.  Slot 2:00 is the single closest known
d49 observation to the test start (2:15), making it a powerful anchor:
- d49_at_2_0          : d49 demand at exactly timestamp 2:0 per geo
- d49_last2_mean      : mean of d49 at timestamps 1:45 and 2:0 per geo

** Ratio Features  **
- ratio_at_hour       : linear extrapolation of d49/d48 ratio to the
                        target hour — THE key signal
- ratio_at_h2         : ratio pinned at hour 2 (most recent anchor)
- ratio_slope         : slope direction of the ratio trend per geohash
- day_ratio           : simple early-mean d49/d48 ratio (geo fallback)
- geo4_ratio          : geo4-level ratio (sparser-geo fallback)

** Scaled Prediction Features **
- d48_day_ratio_pred      : d48_demand × day_ratio
- d48_hour_ratio_pred     : d48_demand × ratio_at_hour
- d48_geo4_ratio_pred     : d48_demand × geo4_ratio
- geo_hour_ratio_pred     : geo_hour_mean × day_ratio
- geo4_hour_ratio_pred    : geo4_hour_mean × geo4_ratio
- geo_hour_hratio_pred    : geo_hour_mean × ratio_at_hour

** Geo × Hour Historical Statistics (from d48) **
- geo_hour_mean/std/med/q25/q75  per (geohash, hour)
- geo_d48_mean/std/med            per geohash
- geo4_hour_mean                  per (geo4, hour)
- geo3_hour_mean                  per (geo3, hour)

** D49 Trend Features  **
- d49_trend        : linear slope of d49 demand across observed hours
- d49_last_demand  : last observed d49 demand value per geohash
- d49_extrapolated : trend projection to target hour

** Structural Features **
- d48_day_slope      : linear slope of d48 demand across all day hours,
                       per geohash (captures rising vs. falling geos)
- geo4_neighbor_mean : mean d48 demand of geo4 siblings excluding self
                       (spatial smoothing for sparse geohashes)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Algorithm : LightGBM (LGBMRegressor)
Objective : regression (RMSE)

Key parameters:
  learning_rate     = 0.015   (slower, more stable)
  num_leaves        = 511
  n_estimators      = 4000    (compensates lower learning rate)
  min_child_samples = 20      (mild regularisation)
  feature_fraction  = 0.8
  bagging_fraction  = 0.8, freq=5
  reg_alpha=0.05, reg_lambda=0.1
  early_stopping    = 150 rounds

Cross-validation : 5-fold KFold with shuffle

Sample weighting : Day-49 rows are upweighted 9× relative to Day-48
rows, because the evaluation metric is R² on Day 49 predictions only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
SEED ENSEMBLE 
------------------------------------------------------------------------
LightGBM with bagging exhibits non-trivial variance between runs.  We
train the full 5-fold CV with 5 different random seeds:
  SEEDS = [42, 7, 123, 314, 999]

This gives 5 × 5 = 25 models.  Final predictions are the mean of all
25 model outputs.  Seed averaging reduces run-to-run variance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
POST-PROCESSING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A soft blend is applied between the ensemble output and the rule-based
d48_hour_ratio_pred baseline:

  final_pred = 0.95 × model_pred + 0.05 × d48_hour_ratio_pred

Both sides are clipped to [0, 1] before blending.  The blend is only
applied if it improves the OOF d49 R²; otherwise raw predictions are
used.  The 5% weight was chosen to anchor extreme extrapolations without
distorting the well-calibrated model output.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
VALIDATION METRIC
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary proxy: R² on Day-49 OOF rows only (mirrors the leaderboard).
We report both R²_all and R²_d49.  All version comparisons use R²_d49.
